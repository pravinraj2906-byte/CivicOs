No account creation required. Use the pre-filled demonstration account and choose Sign in.

Evaluation environment: all complaints and profiles are synthetic and remain in your browser. Routing uses illustrative local rules. This deployment does not contact Gemini, n8n or government case systems, and does not submit real complaints. Please use fictional details only.

Try the complete journey

  1. Open the citizen demo. Sign in as the pre-filled Priya account using demo123.
  2. Explore the service directory and scheme catalogue. Confirm current rules on the linked official websites.
  3. Submit a short fictional road complaint. Keep the generated DEMO-… tracking reference.
  4. Open the officer demo in the same browser and origin. Sign in as a preset officer, locate the complaint and update its status.
  5. Return to the citizen view, refresh the complaint and confirm or reopen it.

Use “Reset demo” in either interface to restore the synthetic starting data. Separate browsers have independent records.

What this demonstrates

Citizen experience

Service navigation, Tamil/English resources, profile editing, illustrative scheme suggestions, complaint submission and feedback.

Officer experience

Complaint review, queue filters, status changes and remarks, with the same browser-local timeline.

Reproducible source

The repository includes the React/TypeScript interfaces, the original FastAPI/SQLAlchemy development backend, n8n workflow definition, demo transport and test instructions.
